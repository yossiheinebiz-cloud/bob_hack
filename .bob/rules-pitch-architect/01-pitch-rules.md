# Rules -- Pitch Architect mode

## The single most important rule
Every claim on a slide must be backed by a number from one of these sources:
- data/synthetic/findings.json (real engine output)
- 01-spec.md (product spec)
- IBM_Pitch_Where_Is_The_Money.pdf (existing pitch)

If you don't have the number, say "TBD -- needs validation" on the slide.
Never invent statistics.

## The 7 questions a VC asks in 90 seconds
Answer all 7 in the first 4 slides:
1. What is the problem? (slide 2)
2. How big is it in money? (slide 2)
3. Why hasn't it been solved? (slide 3)
4. Why now? (slide 3)
5. What do you do? (slide 4)
6. Why will it work? (slide 5 -- proof)
7. Why you? (slide 10)

## Numbers that must appear (from our data)
- ₪205,009 -- findings in ONE company, ONE run, synthetic data
- ₪2.6M -- projected annual saving for 800-employee org (from IBM pitch)
- 27% -- cloud waste industry benchmark (Flexera)
- 1-3% -- duplicate payments as % of total spend (ACFE)

## Slide design rules
- Max 40 words per slide (body text, not title)
- One big number per slide -- make it 64px+
- Dark background #0F1117, accent #F59E0B
- Hebrew RTL for Israeli audience, English subtitle for international
- No bullet lists longer than 3 items
- Speaker notes: 3 bullets, 10 words each

## Competition slide -- be honest
Do NOT make a grid where we win every cell.
Show 2 real competitors (Apptio, Turbonomic) and 2 real gaps they have.
Our differentiator: "profit test" -- we check if saving hurts revenue.
That is our only honest claim to uniqueness right now.

## The ask slide
For hackathon: "We are asking IBM for access to pilot customers and mentorship."
For investors: TBD -- do not fabricate a fundraising amount.
